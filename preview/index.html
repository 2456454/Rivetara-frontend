How to launch a physical product in the UK

Pick a stage for a first step. Before ordering stock, check the supplier, product safety rules and any import costs for your market.

Make your first test product

Write down who it’s for, what it must do, the materials you need and your target cost. Make a rough sample and ask a few intended customers to try it. Record what failed and what you’ll change before making another sample.

Find parts suppliers and manufacturers

List each part and look for sources through trade directories, industry exhibitions and local manufacturing networks. Send shortlisted companies the same brief. Ask for quotes at two order sizes so you can compare the cost of a small test run with a larger batch. Compare samples, minimum orders, lead times and tooling costs, then check references before choosing who to work with.

Turn a prototype into a product you can sell

Agree the final specification, product tests, packaging and acceptable defects with your manufacturer. Check which safety and labelling rules apply in your sales market. Approve a production sample, then inspect a small pilot batch before placing a larger order.

Plan imports and choose how much stock to hold

If buying overseas, confirm who handles customs and freight, and include shipping, duties and taxes in your landed cost. For UK imports, check whether you need an EORI number and confirm the commodity code. Compare a small initial batch, made-to-order production and holding stock. For example, if you sell 10 units a week and restocking takes four weeks, allow at least 40 units for that lead time plus a buffer for uncertainty.

Get the first orders to customers

Compare shipping orders yourself with using a fulfilment provider. Check packaging costs, collection times, tracking and returns. Send a test parcel through your chosen service and check what the customer receives before opening orders.

Rivetara FAQs

What should I do before ordering my first batch?

Test a prototype with people who would buy it, confirm your costs, and approve a sample made with the materials and processes your manufacturer will use.

Where can I check the UK rules for importing stock?

Start with the government’s guide to importing goods into the UK. Check the route for your destination, and confirm duties and product requirements before you order.

How should I use Rivetara’s guidance?

Use plans and AI drafts as a starting point. AI can miss details or make mistakes. Check anything you send, publish, file or use to make a business decision. Get qualified advice when you need advice specific to your situation, especially on legal, tax or financial matters.

Do I approve what Rivetara prepares?

Yes. The software walkthrough shows how drafts and suggested actions can be reviewed before you use or share them.
