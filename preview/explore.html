Is this just for the paperwork?

The Rivetara vision goes beyond admin: supporting the journey from winning work to organising finances and planning growth. This front-page preview illustrates that direction; it is not a confirmed feature or availability list.

Do I stay in control of my business?

Yes. The experience is designed around preparing work for your review, with clear opportunities to check details and choose your next step.

What does tax support mean?

Helping organise expenses and business records for tax preparation. Tax advice and filing depend on your circumstances and should be handled with a qualified professional. This preview does not submit returns.
